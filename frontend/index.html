Connect your Android phone

  1. Phone plugged in, File transfer on

    Plug the phone into this Mac with a USB cable and unlock it. On the phone, open the USB notification and choose File transfer. If you don't see these options, unplug the cable and plug it back in.

  2. Phone free for FlashTransfer

    Photos, Image Capture or another app may be using the phone. FlashTransfer asks before changing anything.

  3. Connection opened

    FlashTransfer starts talking to the phone. This can take a few seconds.

  4. Phone unlocked and sharing files

    Keep the phone unlocked with its USB mode on File transfer, not Charging only. If it asks Allow access to phone data?, tap Allow.
